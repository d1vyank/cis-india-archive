Amnesty International is in the midst of designing its next big global strategy and were seeking inputs to 5 key questions (attached here). They were soliciting provocations to make sure that future of work issues are not left out of their vision and future strategy. Also invited was Will Stronge, director of Autonomy, a think tank in the UK. While he spoke about Autonomy's work that looks at future of work as enmeshed with climate change issues, we brought in a global South perspective and also made a case for why looking at gender is crucial, and why there is a need for methodological innovation in the study of the future of work.
