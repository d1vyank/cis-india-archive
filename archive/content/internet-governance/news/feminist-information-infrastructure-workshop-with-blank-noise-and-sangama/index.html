The workshop focused around fleshing out the research projects that interns at Blank Noise and Sangama will be undertaking, and going over ethical guidelines for feminist research for interviews.

There will be three projects across the two organizations, looking at

  1. The identification process and challenges therein for the trans community,
  2. The impact of mobile phone usage on sex work, and
  3. Impact of social media on the lives of sexual minorities (specifically gay men), focusing on pleasure, social interaction, safety, and peerveillance.
