The event focused on the political economy of reform in India, foreign and security policy, and the role of innovation and technology in India's internal transformation and international relations.

Core aims of the workshop included:

  • Share and debate views on what changes we expect in the value of data over next decade.
  • Challenge and explore the underlying drivers of change across broad arena.
  • Debate the regional and global perspectives and highlight unique issues of greatest impact.
  • Build an informed collective view on the topic for all to use going forward.
