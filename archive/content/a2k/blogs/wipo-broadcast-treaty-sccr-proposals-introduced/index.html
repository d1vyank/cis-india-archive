At the ongoing session of the 26th SCCR, India and the United States have introduced proposals for discussion:

  1. India introduced this proposal for Article 5 (Definitions)
  2. India introduced this proposal for Article 6 (Scope of Application)
  3. Japan introduced this proposal as Article 6 bis
  4. India introduced this proposal for Article 7 (Beneficiaries of Protection)
  5. India introduced this proposal for Article 9 (Protection for Broadcasting Organizations)
  6. The United States introduced a Proposal for Discussion for Article 9 (Protection for Broadcasting Organizations). The delegation was keen to clarify that this was not a “US Proposal”; but merely a “Proposal for Discussion”
