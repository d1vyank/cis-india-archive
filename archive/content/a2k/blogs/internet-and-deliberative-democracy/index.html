Sunil Abraham, director of the Centre for Internet and Society in Bangalore expresses previously unpublished views on piracy, attribution and the IP regime in a panel discussion on Internet and Deliberative Democracy with Philippe Aigrain (Sopinspace), and Prof. Mario Losano (jurist, Univ. Piemonte Orientale) moderated by J.C. De Martin of NEXA Center for Internet and Society at the premier edition of the Democracy Biennial, Torino May 25 2009.
