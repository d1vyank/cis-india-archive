The workshop agenda included:

  • Increasing the reach of Odia Wikipedia in western Odisha
  • Bringing new editors on board
  • Orientation of students from Sambalpur University
  • Exploring possibilities of CIS partnership with Sambalpur University
  • Getting in place an active community on WikiTungi in Sambalpur
