Seamless mobility in today's world is critically linked to how good the map on your mobile device is. Moving around in a car is no different from navigating your journey on a map. The creator of the ground breaking Google Maps, is now planning to change the future of new mobility. Brian heads the research centre for developing new products at Uber, the global behemoth that has disrupted traditional urban mobility. Uber’s need for advanced and accurate mapping of geographies is crucial to its business and Brian's thoughts on the future of Mapping & Mobility will provide valuable insight on smart and secure mobility, which is a core part of the proposed smart cities project initiated by the Government of India.
