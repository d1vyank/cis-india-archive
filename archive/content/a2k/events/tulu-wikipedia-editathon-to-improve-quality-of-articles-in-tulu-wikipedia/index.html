Dr. Kishore Kumar Rai, Principal of Shri Ramakrishna PU College, who himself is an active Tulu and Kannada Wikipedian provided the lab for this activity. Any Wikipedian (Tulu/Kannada) can walk-in and make use of the lab to edit Wikipedia. He has also procured more than 100 Tulu books from Tulu Sahitya Academy which will be kept there for reference.

Agenda

  • Improve the quality of articles in Tulu Wikipedia
  • Increase the length of short articles
  • Add inter-wiki links
  • Add references
  • Add images
  • Add categories
  • Improve language

The event will continue in June. More details can be accessed here.
