In this context, applications under the RTI Act have been sent to departments covering persons with disabilities in all states requesting the following details for the period 2012 – 2014:

  1. Total budget allocated for each scheme per year.
  2. Total expenditure on budget for each scheme per year.
  3. List of beneficiaries for each scheme per year.

Responses are currently being received from various districts and state departments for the above queries. State-wise details will be compiled and published on the website.
