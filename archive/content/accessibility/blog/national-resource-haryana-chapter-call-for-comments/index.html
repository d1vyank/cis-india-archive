Introduction

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment is responsible for the welfare of persons with disabilities in Haryana. The state has an estimated population of 4,55,040 persons with disabilities in a total population of 25,353,081.

Haryana implements two schemes under Women and Children with Disabilities, three schemes under Education, three schemes under Skill Development and Employment, and five schemes under Social Security, Health, Rehabilitation and Recreation.

1.    General Entitlements

Haryana does not implement any scheme / policy that can be listed in this category.

2.    Women & Children with Disabilities

2.1    Financial assistance is provided to children with disabilities not attending school due to their disability.

2.2    Haryana provides financial assistance to children of persons with disabilities.

6.    Accessibility

6.1 The government of Haryana is yet to implement schemes and policies under accessibility.

7.    Certification

7.1 Haryana has not identified any hospital / medical officer for issueing of disability certificates.

8.    Schemes & Grants for Organisations & Individuals for Disabilities

8.1    The government of Haryana is yet to implement schemes and policies under this category.

9.    Disability Authorities

Haryana has two authorities responsible for the welfare of persons with disabilities:

  • Department of Social Justice and Empowerment
  • Commissioner for Disabilities
