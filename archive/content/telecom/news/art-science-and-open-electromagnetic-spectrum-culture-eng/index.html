Podcast

Sharath Chandra Ram describes his work in art, design and open science, and information culture. He maintains the Signal Lab  at the Srishti Institute of Art Design and Technology in Bangalore, India. He describes his workshop in open radio astronomy that he held at the International Astronomical Union Commission C2, at the Parque Explora in Medellin, Colombia and his work in telecom policy and with the indian fishing communities embroiled in ownership of the electromagnetic spectrum debates –  In dialogue with Roger Malina.
