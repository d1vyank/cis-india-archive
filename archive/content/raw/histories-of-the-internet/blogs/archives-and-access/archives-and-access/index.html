--- title: Archives and Access date: '2011-09-22' authors: - Prasad Krishna tags: - RAW Publications - Publications - Histories of Internet - Researchers at Work - Internet Histories - Archives description: The monograph by Aparna Balachandran and Rochelle Pinto, is a material history of the Internet archives. It examines the role of the archivist and the changing relationship between the state and private archives for looking at the politics of subversion, preservation and value of archiving. By examining the Tamil Nadu and Goa state archives, along with the larger public and state archives in the country, the monograph looks at the materiality of archiving, the ambitions and aspirations of an archive, and why it is necessary to preserve archives, not as historical artefacts but as living interactive spaces of memory and remembrance. The findings have direct implications on various government and market impulses to digitise archives and show a clear link between opening up archives and other knowledge sources for breathing life into local and alternative histories. url: /raw/histories-of-the-internet/blogs/archives-and-access/archives-and-access/ original_url: https://cis-india.org/raw/histories-of-the-internet/blogs/archives-and-access/archives-and-access type: blog program: raw image: /files/raw/histories-of-the-internet/blogs/archives-and-access/archives-and-access/image.jpeg ---
