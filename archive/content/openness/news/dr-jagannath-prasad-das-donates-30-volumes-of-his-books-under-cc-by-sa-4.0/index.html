Earlier Bhubaneswar based non-profit Srujanika had donated over 700 books. Two hundred and nineteen books including entire Odia Bhagabata and many other classics have already been digitised on Odia Wikisource. The collection is growing slowly with more books being digitised by volunteer Wikisourcers.
