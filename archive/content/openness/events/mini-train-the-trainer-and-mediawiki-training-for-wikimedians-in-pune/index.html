Agenda (for Mediawiki training):

  • Bug triage
  • Bots
  • Mediawiki extensions and scripts
  • Wikidata

Depending on the community needs, we will balance the depth of each topic.

Agenda (for Train-the-Trainer training):

  • Discussion with the community to come up with ideas on topics such as planning, organising and reporting outreach
  • Public speaking
  • Advanced editing skills
  • New grants structure.
