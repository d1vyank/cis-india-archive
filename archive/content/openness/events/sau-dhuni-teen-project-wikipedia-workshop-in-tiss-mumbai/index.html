--- title: '"Sau Dhuni Teen" project: Wikipedia workshop in TISS, Mumbai' date: '2015-08-22' description: A two-day multilingual Wikipedia workshop is being planned to be organised at the Women's Studies Department of Tata Institute of Social Sciences, Mumbai during the 22nd through the 24th August. url: /openness/events/sau-dhuni-teen-project-wikipedia-workshop-in-tiss-mumbai/ original_url: https://cis-india.org/openness/events/sau-dhuni-teen-project-wikipedia-workshop-in-tiss-mumbai type: event program: openness event: start: '2015-08-22T00:00:00+00:00' end: '2015-08-24T00:00:00+00:00' where: Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai contact_name: Abhinav Garule --- Under the scope of the "Sau Dhuni Teen" project, student and faculty volunteers of TISS will contribute in creating/editing Wikipedia articles in about notable people, books and concepts relating to women's studies, gender studies, and more broadly, interdisciplinary social sciences. The event is being organised in collaboration with Centre for Indian Languages in Higher Education (CILHE).
