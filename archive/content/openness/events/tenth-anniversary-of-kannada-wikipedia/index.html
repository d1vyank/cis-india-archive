--- title: Tenth Anniversary of Kannada Wikipedia date: '2013-11-17' description: Kannada Wikipedia has completed 10 years. The Kannada Wikipedia community will be celebrating the tenth anniversary of Kannada Wikpedia on Nov 17, 2013 from 9.30 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. at H N Multimedia Hall, National College, Basavanagudi, Bangalore. CIS-A2K is a co-sponsor for the program. url: /openness/events/tenth-anniversary-of-kannada-wikipedia/ original_url: https://cis-india.org/openness/events/tenth-anniversary-of-kannada-wikipedia type: event program: openness event: start: '2013-11-17T09:30:00+00:00' end: '2013-11-17T13:30:00+00:00' where: H N Multimedia Hall, National College, Basavanagudi, Bangalore contact_name: Dr. U.B.Pavanaja ---

The guests of honour will be Dr. U R Ananthamurthy, Prof. G. Venkatasubbiah and Ravi Hegde. There will be speeches by the chief guests from 10.00 a.m. to 11.00 a.m. Dr. U.B. Pavanaja. This will be followed by a talk by some Kannada Wikipedians. At the end there will be a edit-a-thon where Wikipedians will add some articles to Kannada Wikipedia.
