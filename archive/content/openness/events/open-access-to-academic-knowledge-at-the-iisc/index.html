The workshop will involve an introductory presentation by Dr Francis Jayakanth of the National Centre for Science Information, followed by an interactive discussion of Open Access issues and ideas. This is a free event.

The workshop will take place in the Seminar Hall of the National Centre for Science Information, within the Indian Institute of Science from 14:30 to 17:00. For a map to this event click here.
