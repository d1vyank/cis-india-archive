Calling all hackers, designers, makers, the open source community, space enthusiasts and the ones who will join us for the main NASA Space Apps Challenge Hackathon.

We are thrilled to share with you that members of Hillhacks would join us to talk about the amazing work they have done so far and about their upcoming hill hacks too.

Also joining would be OpenCube Labs to share their experience of their projects in previous editions of the NASA Space Apps Challenge.

This pre-event will be a great place for you to get your doubts out of the way about the main hackathon.

You possibly can meet potential team members who can give you additional dimensions for your project. You can learn how you can best leverage the data available from NASA and tackle the challenges by them.

See you there!
