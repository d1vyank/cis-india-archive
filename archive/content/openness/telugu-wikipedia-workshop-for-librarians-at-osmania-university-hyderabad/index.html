--- title: Telugu Wikipedia workshop for librarians at Osmania University, Hyderabad date: '2016-01-11' description: This 11th, a Telugu Wikipedia workshop for librarians is going to be organised at Osmania University, Hyderabad, Telengana. Telugu Wikimedia community and the Centre for Internet and Soceity's Access to Knowledge programme (CIS-A2K) are collaborating in helping librarians learn about improving Wikipedia content related to publication and library science. Telugu Wikimedian Bhaskara Naidu is leading the event with support from Academician and Librarian Dr. Achala Munigal. CIS-A2K member Pavan Santosh will be presenting about the Telugu WikiProjects for collaborating with libraries and would be coordinating the event. url: /openness/telugu-wikipedia-workshop-for-librarians-at-osmania-university-hyderabad/ original_url: https://cis-india.org/openness/telugu-wikipedia-workshop-for-librarians-at-osmania-university-hyderabad type: event program: openness event: start: '2016-01-11T10:00:00+00:00' end: '2016-01-11T17:50:00+00:00' where: Osmania University, Hyderabad, Telengana --- More details in event page on Telugu Wikipedia.
