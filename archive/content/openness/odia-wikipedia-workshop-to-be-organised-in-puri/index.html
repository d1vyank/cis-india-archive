This workshop aims at helping the participants learn about creating and improving the Odia Wikipedia articles about the local places, people, and culture of the historical town Puri and the district. Currently there is some presence of the town and the district on Odia Wikipedia but majority of the content are related only to Jagannath temple. The participants will be oriented with Wikipedia's basics, policy and guidelines of Odia Wikipedia, Odia-alphabet input. The event is free and open to everyone.
