Professor Subbiah Arunachalam has been called "India's and the Developing World's great advocate for Open Access". He has worked in the field for over ten years, holding workshops, trainings, and political and policy initiatives.

In this interview we talk about how to improve the current state of Open Access, what the Indian Open Access experience might teach the rest of the world, and Arunachalam's view on the future of the Open Access movement.
