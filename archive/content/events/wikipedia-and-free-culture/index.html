Bangalore International Centre, in collaboration with the Centre for Internet and Society, 

cordially invites you to an interactive discussion

Wikipedia and Free Culture

with

Jimmy "Jimbo" Wales

Founder, Wikipedia

and

Sue Gardner

Executive Director, Wikimedia Foundation

Jimmy Wales, founder of Wikipedia, will deliver a presentation about the history, growth, and prominence of Wikipedia on the internet today. Since its inception in 2001, Wikipedia has risen quickly to become the largest information resource and free knowledge repository in history, now with over 11 million articles in over 250 unique language editions.

Sue Gardner, Executive Director of the Wikimedia Foundation, will speak on the origins of the Wikimedia Foundation, the San Francisco based non profit organisation that operates Wikimedia and other free knowledge projects.

Lawrence Liang, member, Alternative Law Forum, and author of A Guide to Open Content Licences, will steer the discussions.
